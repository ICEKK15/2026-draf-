PLAYER VALUE

Exact ESPN custom projections drive the board

Where you supplied ESPN 2026 projections, the exact ESPN FPTS value is the production baseline. Standalone value uses normalized VORP plus positional projection strength. ESPN ADP/rank affects availability timing only; it does not make a player better or worse.

EXPERT TAG

Aggregated research label

The Expert column summarizes cumulative evidence into labels such as BREAKOUT, VALUE, SLEEPER, FADE, WAIT, UP, DOWN or HOLD. Intel is descriptive first and can only break a genuinely close objective comparison; it cannot override a clear projection/value edge.

DECISION MODEL

Positional advantage + live scarcity

Custom projections and positional value are primary. Live scarcity and wait cost determine timing. QB3 urgency activates only when the pool is thinning or critical, so the app can reach for a strong third QB without giving quarterbacks a hidden second scoring bonus.

LATE ROUNDS

Exactly one K and one D/ST — at the end

The engine blocks K and D/ST from recommendations until your final two roster selections and will never recommend a second kicker or second defense. Earlier bench picks stay focused on asymmetric RB/WR/TE upside.
